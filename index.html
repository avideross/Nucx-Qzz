About NUCX Group – Bawal

Powering industrial workforce across Bawal and Rewari.

NUCX Group is a manpower sourcing and recruitment network focused on connecting job seekers, industrial companies, manpower contractors and recruitment partners.

Our primary focus is the industrial employment sector in Bawal and Rewari, Haryana. We aim to make the recruitment process simpler by connecting suitable candidates with relevant job opportunities and helping manpower agencies meet their workforce requirements.

Our Mission

To connect people with employment opportunities and support industrial businesses through organised manpower sourcing.

Our Vision

To build a reliable recruitment and manpower partner network across industrial locations.

Important Note: We believe in transparency and responsible recruitment. We do not promise guaranteed selection or employment. Candidates are not required to pay any registration fee to NUCX Group.
